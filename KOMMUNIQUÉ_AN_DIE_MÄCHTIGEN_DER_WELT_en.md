## COMMUNICATION TO THE POWERFUL OF THE WORLD

**Re: The Funnel Junction – A call to collectively widen the opening angle**

**From:** [Trichterwächter:in]
Dear leaders of this world,

This is no ordinary appeal. It stems from no ideology, no nation, no economic interest group. It arises from an insight grounded in physics and cybernetics, which we summarize in a concise image.

**I. The Diagnosis**

The future of humanity resembles an open funnel. For the first time in history, its opening is determined not only by humans but significantly by automata: by algorithms, AI systems, and autonomous technologies that control our financial markets, our weapon systems, and our public spaces.

Complexity theory teaches us that such systems generate inherently uncertain paths. There is no predetermined outcome. But there is a critical parameter: **the opening angle of the funnel.**

- **If this angle is narrow**, humanity's path will most likely lead to self-annihilation – through escalation, through misunderstanding, or through a single algorithmic error that no one can correct.

- **If this angle is wide**, the path remains open for a peaceful, diverse, and hopeful future.

**II. The Junction**

We assert: Humanity reaches this junction **now**. Not in ten years. Not after the next election. But in this historical moment.

You, the leaders of the great power centers, can still help shape this angle.

You can still talk to each other – not to settle old conflicts, but to negotiate the shape of the funnel.

You can still reach an agreement that widens the opening angle enough to prevent humanity from plunging into the abyss.

**III. The Action**

We demand:

1. **An immediate, non-public security cabinet of the powerful** – without protocol, without publicity, without loss of face. A purely technical body that controls the automata, not the people.

2. **A public conference in the spirit of Plato's Symposium** – not to conquer, but to understand. With thinkers from all cultures, all disciplines, all levels of power.

3. **A binding treaty limiting the control corridors of automata** – analogous to the arms control treaties of the Cold War, but for algorithms. A treaty that defines the areas in which humans **must** decide, even if it is inefficient.

**IV. The Final Plea**

You have the power to close the funnel – or to open it wider. There is no neutral position. Those who do not speak let the automata speak. Those who do not act leave the opening angle to chance – and chance is no friend of humanity.

Humanity does not ask you to love each other. It asks you to keep the funnel open.

With the utmost urgency,

[Trichterwächter:in]
